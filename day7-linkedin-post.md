Day 7 of #10DaysOfVoiceAgents is live.

Today I taught my voice agent when to stop trying to solve everything on its own and ask for human help.

It now creates a short escalation summary only after asking for permission, keeps the details focused, and stores requests in a local dashboard so a human can review them.

This build uses Murf Falcon for the voice layer, and I’m proud to be building with Murf AI as part of the challenge.
